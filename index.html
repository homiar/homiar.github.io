About

I study how AI and digital health tools can make healthcare clearer and safer for patients and clinicians.

I am a researcher in the Division of Clinical Informatics and the Department of General Medicine at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center and Harvard Medical School, focused on developing and evaluating AI-driven digital health tools for patient engagement, health communication, and clinical decision-making. I coordinate multi-site studies in remote patient monitoring, wearable technologies, and telehealth.

I am also a Master of Medical Science (MMSc) student in Biomedical Informatics at the Blavatnik Institute of Biomedical Informatics at Harvard Medical School. Previously, I served as a Research Assistant in the Department of Psychiatry at the University of Oxford, where I led the design and validation of large language model prompts to automate evidence synthesis for living systematic reviews in the Wellcome-funded GALENOS project, achieving 100% sensitivity with up to 86% less screening effort. Before that, I earned my Honors Bachelor of Science in Life Sciences from McMaster University.

My broader research focuses on developing and evaluating AI for patient-facing care and evidence synthesis. Beyond conversational clinical AI, my work explores large language models for screening, risk-of-bias assessment, and data extraction in systematic reviews; text mining of the biomedical literature to detect emerging research trends; and digital phenotyping using smartphones and wearables.

During my time at the Center for Addiction and Mental Health and St. Joseph's Healthcare Hamilton, I collaborated with clinicians and researchers on digital mental health, including Google search histories for early detection of suicidal behavior, wearable sensing for addiction, biomarkers for depression care, and digital technology use and cognitive therapy for schizophrenia. I have also held a research role at St. Michael's Hospital and completed training in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and the ethics of emerging technologies at the University of Oxford and the London School of Economics.
